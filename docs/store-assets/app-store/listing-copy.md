**Description** (≤4000):
```
Containment has failed. A revival-biotech outpost lost control of its specimens — dinosaurs that integrated the cloning hardware into themselves and no longer answer to anyone. From a fixed top-down camera, you pilot a hero mech alongside chains of sci-fi turrets and hold the line. Every run ends in extinction — yours or theirs.

PILOT A HERO, NOT JUST A CURSOR
Four pilotable mechs — George, Leela, Mike, Stan — each with four active abilities: dash, burst, self-buff, and a pilot-specific ultimate, fighting in real time on the same map as your towers. Unlock them with bolts dropped per kill.

SIX TOWER TYPES
Pulse, Chain, Cryo, Mortar, Pyre, and Hive — a drone bay whose drones orbit assigned towers to boost their fire rate. Two upgrade paths each, three tiers per path.

SIX MATRIARCH QUEENS
Biome-themed bosses, each with her own model, resistance profile, and child-spawn stream. The raptor queen sheds juvenile swarms as she runs; the apex apatosaur ends the campaign.

THIRTY OUTPOSTS, SIX BIOMES
Hand-designed levels across forest, snow, desert, wasteland, lava, and alien biomes. Eight enemy kinds with distinct speeds, HP, and resistances, layered with shield, heal, regen, elite, and fierce buff chips.

HEROIC + CONTAINMENT MODIFIERS
Each level grades up to three stars on Normal, plus a bonus star for Heroic and one for Containment. Five stars per level. Endless mode opens after the final outpost.

No ads. No microtransactions. Single-player. The full campaign is also free in any browser at play.mesozoicprotocol.com.
```
