## Description tab

### Short Description (≤300 chars)


### About This Game (Steam BBCode — paste into the rich-text field's source view)
```
[b]Containment has failed.[/b] A revival-biotech outpost lost control of its specimens — dinosaurs that integrated the cloning hardware into themselves and no longer answer to anyone. From a fixed top-down camera, you pilot a hero mech alongside chains of sci-fi turrets and hold the line. Every run ends in extinction — yours or theirs.

[h2]Pilot a hero, not just a cursor[/h2]
Four pilotable mechs — George, Leela, Mike, Stan — each with four active abilities: dash, burst, self-buff, and a pilot-specific ultimate, fighting in real time on the same map as your towers. Unlock them with bolts dropped per kill.

[h2]Six tower types[/h2]
Pulse, Chain, Cryo, Mortar, Pyre, and Hive — a drone bay whose drones orbit assigned towers to boost their fire rate. Two upgrade paths each, three tiers per path, plus a persistent meta-skill tree per tower kind.

[h2]Six matriarch queens[/h2]
Biome-themed bosses, each with her own model, resistance profile, and child-spawn stream. The raptor queen sheds juvenile swarms as she runs; the apex apatosaur ends the campaign.

[h2]Thirty outposts, six biomes[/h2]
Hand-designed levels across forest, snow, desert, wasteland, lava, and alien biomes. Eight enemy kinds with distinct speeds, HP, and resistances, layered with shield, heal, regen, elite, and fierce buff chips.

[h2]Heroic + Containment modifiers[/h2]
Each level grades up to three stars on Normal, plus a bonus star for Heroic (forbidden tower or rewritten waves) and one for Containment (one life, locked loadout, no selling). Five stars per level. Endless mode opens after the final outpost.

[h2]Free on the web[/h2]
The full campaign is free to play in any modern browser at [url=https://play.mesozoicprotocol.com]play.mesozoicprotocol.com[/url] — no ads, no microtransactions, no accounts. This Steam build is the desktop version.
```
