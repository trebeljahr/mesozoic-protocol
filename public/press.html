Three paragraphs

Mesozoic Protocol is a single-player roguelite tower defense from Ricos Labs LLC, the studio of Berlin-based solo developer Rico Trebeljahr. Players take the role of an operator at a revival-biotech outpost where containment has very much failed: the cloning rigs are still running inside the perimeter, and the specimens have integrated the revival hardware into themselves. From a fixed top-down camera, the player pilots a hero mech alongside chains of sci-fi turrets and holds the line through six biomes and thirty hand-designed outposts.

The game pairs two layers that most TDs keep separate. The tower layer is six classic-feeling kinds — Pulse, Chain, Cryo, Mortar, Pyre (flame), and Hive (drone support that buffs adjacent turrets) — each with two upgrade paths and three tiers, plus a persistent meta-skill tree per tower kind. The hero layer is four piloted mechs with full QWER kits — dash, burst, self-buff, pilot-specific ultimate — unlocked with bolts, the in-game scrap currency dropped per enemy kill: George (kinetic sniper) at 0 bolts, Leela (electric skirmisher) at 250, Mike (flame) at 600, Stan (heavy artillery) at 1,200. Pilot ultimates are distinct: George's Bullet Storm (fire-rate frenzy), Leela's Storm Surge (self-AoE chain lightning), Mike's Inferno Ring (expanding flame waves), Stan's Annihilator Missile (single huge warhead). Bosses are six biome-themed matriarch queens — raptor, stego, para, allosaur, armored, and the apex apatosaur — each with her own model, resistance profile, and child-spawn stream. Heroic and Containment level modifiers stack extra stars on top of the base three-star grade.

The full campaign is free to play in any modern browser at protocol.trebeljahr.com — no ads, no microtransactions, no accounts. Desktop builds for Windows, macOS, and Linux, plus iOS and Android ports, are in preparation, with planned single-tier pricing of $6.99 on desktop storefronts and $3.99 on mobile.

Key Features

  • A piloted hero mech. Four pilots — George (kinetic sniper), Leela (electric skirmisher), Mike (flame), Stan (heavy artillery) — each with a four-slot QWER kit: dash, burst, self-buff, pilot-specific ultimate (Bullet Storm / Storm Surge / Inferno Ring / Annihilator Missile). Heroes unlock with bolts dropped per kill: 0 / 250 / 600 / 1,200.
  • Six tower types, two upgrade paths each, three tiers per path: Pulse, Chain, Cryo, Mortar, Pyre (flame), and Hive (drone support that buffs adjacent towers).
  • Eight enemy kinds with distinct speeds, HP, damage, and resistances — raptor, allosaur, stego, swarm, armored, para, titan, and the boss queens. Per-spawn buff chips layer shields, healing, regen, elite, and fierce on top.
  • Six biome-themed matriarch bosses: raptor queen, stego matriarch, parasaur resonator, allosaur apex predator, armored ceratopsian, and the apatosaur apex. Each has her own child-spawn stream.
  • Thirty outposts across six biomes — forest, snow, desert, wasteland, lava, alien.
  • Heroic + Containment level modifiers. Each level grades 0–3 stars on Normal plus 1 bonus star for Heroic (forbidden tower or rewritten wave list) and 1 for Containment (one life, locked loadout, no selling). Five stars per level, thirty levels.
  • Persistent meta-skill tree per tower kind. Stars unlock permanent modifiers — Subzero on Cryo, Combustion on Pyre, Annihilator on Pulse, and so on.
  • Per-hero skill tree layered on top of variant baselines.
  • Endless mode, unlocked after clearing the final outpost.
  • Top-down 3D with a fixed orthographic camera, snapped to 90° rotations.
  • Compendium with Heroes, Towers, Enemies, Mechanics, and Credits tabs.
  • Single-player only, no F2P mechanics, no microtransactions.

Background — How and Why

I built Mesozoic Protocol because the tower defense genre is starved for asymmetric enemies and starved for a reason to keep your hands on the keyboard between waves. There are hundreds of TDs and most of them have generic fantasy goblins, and most of them ask the player to watch instead of act.

The hero mech is the answer to both. You're piloting one of four heroes with a full QWER kit — dash, burst, self-buff, pilot-specific ultimate — alongside your towers, in real time, on the same map. Heroes unlock with bolts dropped per kill — so the hero shop is the meta loop and the level grind is what feeds it.

Bosses are biome-themed matriarchs. The raptor queen sheds juvenile swarms behind her as she runs. The stego matriarch calves a stego every few seconds. The parasaur resonator amplifies chain damage. The apex apatosaur at level 30 is the final-wave threat — cold is the only real lever against her. Six queens, six biomes, every encounter a different shape.

I'm building this solo in Berlin with TypeScript, React Three Fiber, Vite, and Tauri. The web build is the canonical version. Audio and 3D assets are licensed from indie marketplaces; everything else is hand-built.

— Rico Trebeljahr

About the Developer

Rico Trebeljahr is a software engineer and solo game developer based in Berlin, and the founder of Ricos Labs LLC. Mesozoic Protocol is his studio's first commercial project. He has previously built and maintained the open-source two-player strategy game Tiao — a digital implementation of an abstract board game designed by Andreas Edmeier, free to play at playtiao.com.

Rico writes about programming, side projects, and games at ricos.site. He builds games in TypeScript using React Three Fiber for rendering, Tauri for desktop distribution, and Capacitor for mobile — a stack that ships the same simulation to web, desktop, and mobile from a single codebase.
