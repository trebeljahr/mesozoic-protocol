Privacy Policy

Mesozoic Protocol — last updated 11 June 2026

This page describes what data Mesozoic Protocol collects and how it is used. The policy applies to the web build at protocol.trebeljahr.com and to the iOS, Android, macOS, Windows, and Linux distributions of the game.

Who runs this

Mesozoic Protocol is built and operated by Ricos Labs LLC. Contact: mesozoic-protocol@ricoslabs.com.

What we collect

Web build (protocol.trebeljahr.com only): we record anonymous, aggregated visit statistics through a self-hosted Plausible Analytics instance at plausible.trebeljahr.com. For each visit we store:

  • The page you visited
  • The site that referred you, if any
  • Your country and browser (derived from the request, not stored as your IP address)
  • A daily-rotating hash used only to count unique visits — discarded after 24 hours

No cookies are set. No IP addresses are stored. No personal identifiers, cross-site tracking, or fingerprinting takes place.

Mobile and desktop apps (iOS, Android, macOS, Windows, Linux): analytics is disabled. The apps do not send gameplay events, telemetry, crash reports, or any other data to us. Whatever statistics you see in the App Store or Play Store come from Apple and Google directly, not from us.

All platforms: your saved games, settings, and unlocks are stored in local browser or device storage (localStorage) on the device that produced them. This data never leaves your device.

What we do not collect

  • Names, email addresses, phone numbers, or any other personal identifiers
  • Account passwords (there are no accounts)
  • Payment information (the game does not sell or transact)
  • Precise or approximate location
  • Contacts, photos, microphone, or camera access
  • Advertising IDs or third-party ad SDK data
  • Crash dumps or device fingerprints

Third parties

The only third-party service involved is Plausible Analytics, which we self-host on infrastructure we operate. No analytics data leaves our infrastructure, and Plausible's privacy model is explicitly cookie-free. The mobile and desktop apps load no third-party services.

Children

Mesozoic Protocol is rated for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any data from children. If you believe a child's data has been collected through this game, please contact us and we will remove it.

Your rights

  • Delete your local data: uninstall the app on mobile or desktop, or clear site data for protocol.trebeljahr.com in your browser. There is no server-side data to delete.
  • Opt out of analytics: install any browser extension that blocks plausible.trebeljahr.com, or use a browser with Do Not Track enabled — Plausible respects DNT by default.
  • Data requests: contact mesozoic-protocol@ricoslabs.com. Since we do not store personal identifiers, we cannot identify your individual visits, but we can confirm what is and is not collected.

Data retention

Aggregated Plausible statistics for the web build are retained indefinitely. They are aggregate counters (visits per page, per country, per day) and cannot be linked back to an individual visitor.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, the "last updated" date at the top of the page will change and, for changes affecting active app users, we will publish a note alongside the next app release. Continuing to use the game after the date changes implies acceptance of the updated policy.
